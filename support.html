Get the space back right away

Deleted photos and videos go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app and stay there for 30 days. To free the space now, open Photos → Utilities → Recently Deleted, tap Select, then Delete All. Items deleted there can't be recovered.

Restore Cleaner Pro on a new device

Open Settings in Cleaner and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

Cancel or change your subscription

In the app: Settings → Manage Subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Cleaner. After cancelling, Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Cancel at least 24 hours before a free trial ends to avoid being charged.

Photos or contacts access

Cleaner needs access to Photos to show and clean up your library, and to Contacts only for the contacts tools. You can change access at any time in Settings → Apps → Cleaner. See the Privacy Policy for how your data is handled.
