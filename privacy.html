Privacy Policy

Cleaner · last updated 20 October 2026

Cleaner does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics services, and no advertising. The app has no server.

Your photos and videos

With your permission, Cleaner reads your photo library on your device to find duplicates, similar shots, bursts, blurry photos, screenshots and large videos. All of this analysis runs on your device. Your photos are never uploaded, copied to a server, or shared.

Cleaner deletes or compresses only what you choose. Every deletion is confirmed by you in a system dialog, and deleted items go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can recover them for 30 days. Compressed copies are saved to your library next to the originals.

Your contacts

With your permission, Cleaner reads your contacts on your device to find duplicates and contacts with no name, phone number or email. Contacts are merged or deleted only when you tap to do so. Your contacts never leave your device. Contacts permission is optional: the rest of the app works without it.

What stays on your device

  • the results of scanning your library and contacts
  • your selections, settings and app icon
  • how much space you have freed with Cleaner

This information is stored only on your device. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

If you subscribe to Cleaner Pro, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, payment details, or Apple Account. The app asks Apple whether a subscription is active and until when — and nothing else. That answer is used on your device to unlock Pro and is never sent to us.

Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Cleaner is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
